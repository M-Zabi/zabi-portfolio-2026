## Design Context

### Users
Founders, product leads and engineering managers evaluating a senior engineer for a
contract or role. They arrive from a referral, a GitHub profile or a LinkedIn message,
usually on a laptop between meetings, sometimes on a phone. They are judging two things
in under a minute: *can this person ship across web, mobile and desktop*, and *does the
work feel as good as it looks*. The job to be done is "decide whether to start a
conversation" — the contact path must be one obvious step from anywhere.

### Brand Personality
**Kinetic, exacting, generous.**
- Kinetic — everything responds. Motion explains state and rewards curiosity; the site
  itself is the strongest portfolio piece for "smooth workflows and interactive experiences".
- Exacting — engineering precision: tabular numerals, hairlines, tight type, nothing jittery.
- Generous — full, saturated colour blocks rather than a timid single accent; warm copy.

### Aesthetic Direction
Colour-block editorial meets a WebGL object. References: trionn.com (dark stage, lit 3D
mark, mono CTAs, orbit lines), minimal numbered service cards with line-art icons, and a
full-bleed acid-yellow testimonial block. Theme follows the system with a manual toggle;
both themes are first-class. Bright blocks (ember, volt, cobalt, mint, blush) keep the same
identity in light and dark — only the neutral canvas inverts.

Anti-references: purple-to-blue gradient SaaS heroes, glassmorphism everywhere, gradient
text, neon-on-black "developer" sites, identical icon-heading-text card grids.

Type: Mona Sans (variable width + weight) for display — the width axis is animated on
interactive links; Schibsted Grotesk for body; Martian Mono only for short labels and
indices.

### Design Principles
1. **One orchestrated moment per surface.** The preloader, the hero reveal and the route
   curtain are the showpieces; micro-interactions stay short (≤ 300ms) and quiet.
2. **Colour is structural, not decorative.** A bright block marks a section or an action;
   it is never sprinkled on text or borders.
3. **Every state is designed.** Loading (preloader, curtain, skeletons), empty, error and
   pending states ship with the feature, not after it.
4. **Motion is optional.** Everything collapses to instant or opacity-only under
   `prefers-reduced-motion`, and the site is fully usable without WebGL.
5. **Touch is not an afterthought.** 44px targets, hover effects gated behind
   `(hover: hover)`, no hover-only information.
